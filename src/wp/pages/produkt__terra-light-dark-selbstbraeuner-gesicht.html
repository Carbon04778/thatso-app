Beschreibung

Multi-position, semi-transparent tanning spray. Moisturising, anti-aging and firming. The lotion has a dual effect:

1. instant natural tanning thanks to a semi-transparent bronze food pigment (no transfer, does not stain clothes)

2. lasting tan that develops between 2 and 3 hours after application (during this time avoid contact with water) and lasts for 3 days.

Smooth and even application, does not require rubbing. Formulation tailored by our researchers to give the skin a healthy and natural color (“tan” effect) without exposure to UV rays. Does not leave spots or an unpleasant odour.
